Report an error in an article

To make a correction request easier to review, include:

  1. The exact link to the article or page.
  2. The title, sentence, command, screenshot or technical detail that you believe is incorrect.
  3. A brief description of the issue and how to reproduce it, if appropriate.
  4. A reliable source, such as vendor documentation, a public advisory, a standards document or an original project release, where possible.

We evaluate actionable correction requests and may update the published article if the evidence supports a change. We do not guarantee an immediate response or any particular outcome.

Report a security problem responsibly

If you discover a potential security issue affecting cyberoplus.com itself, contact us through the email address above. Provide the affected URL, a concise description and the minimum steps needed to understand the behavior.

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, private user information or other secrets. Do not access unrelated accounts, extract third-party data, disrupt services or expand testing beyond authorized activity. A report does not grant permission to perform intrusive assessments.

We ask that you allow a reasonable opportunity for review before publishing details that could expose users or infrastructure to harm. This is a reporting channel, not an invitation to perform unauthorized testing.
