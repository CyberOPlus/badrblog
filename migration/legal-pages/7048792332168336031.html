This Privacy Policy explains how CyberoPlus (www.cyberoplus.com) and the services used to provide the website may collect, use, store or share information when you read our articles, use the site or contact us. It should be read alongside our Cookie Policy.

1. Who operates this website?

CyberoPlus is an independent technology and educational website covering cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, computer systems and practical tutorials. For questions about this policy, privacy requests or information submitted to the site, contact contact@cyberoplus.com or visit our Contact Us page.

2. What information may be processed?

What is processed depends on how you use the website and on which integrated services are active at the time of your visit. Categories may include:

  • Information you choose to send: your email address, name if supplied, message, linked article and any other details you include in a contact or correction request.
  • Technical and usage information: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, referral URL, page visited, request timing, language, general location inferred from an IP address and diagnostic or security events.
  • Cookies and similar identifiers: first-party or third-party cookies, local-storage data, advertising identifiers, consent choices and related technologies, where used by the website or its service providers.
  • Interactions with optional features: information processed by embedded media, forms, advertising, analytics or comments where those features are available.

Not every visitor provides every category of information. An IP address or a technical identifier may constitute personal data under applicable law, even if it does not reveal a visitor's name to us.

3. How information reaches the website

Some information is submitted directly when you send a message. Other information is automatically transmitted as part of normal internet requests to Blogger, Google services, content delivery or security systems and, where enabled, advertising or measurement services. Third-party sites that you open from an article process information under their own policies.

4. Why information may be used

Information may be used to deliver requested pages, keep the website functioning, prevent spam and abuse, diagnose technical problems, respond to messages or correction requests, measure site performance when a measurement service is enabled, and support advertising and associated fraud prevention. Depending on your region, the service and the purpose, processing may require consent or rely on another lawful basis available under applicable law. We do not claim that one legal basis covers every visitor and every processing activity.

5. Blogger and Google's infrastructure

CyberoPlus runs on Blogger, a Google service. Google may receive technical information needed to deliver pages and operate its infrastructure, and Google may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security and other purposes described in its policies. For Google's handling of information, read the Google Privacy Policy.

The site includes Google AdSense advertising integration. Ads may appear depending on the account, page, visitor location and service settings. Google and other participating ad technology providers may place or read cookies in your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers in connection with serving ads, measuring performance, limiting repetition, protecting against fraud and, where allowed, personalizing advertising.

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this or other sites. You can manage Google ad personalization through Google Ads Settings. For certain other advertising vendors, available choices may be described on their own websites or at AboutAds.info.

For a clear explanation of data shared with Google when partner services are used, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The presence of advertising code does not establish that every available ad partner is active for every visitor.

Cookies and similar technologies may support site operation, preferences, security, advertising and measurement. Read our Cookie Policy for categories and ways to control storage. Measurement and performance tools, where enabled, may process site-usage events and technical information to help us understand load failures, browsing patterns or page performance. Specific technologies, retention periods and data transfers depend on the service and its configuration. We do not assert that every optional analytics service is enabled.

Retention depends on the nature of the information, the purpose of processing, account settings, legitimate operational needs and applicable legal obligations. For example, a contact message may be kept long enough to review and respond to it, while technical or advertising data handled by other providers may follow their own retention policies. We cannot give a reliable universal deletion schedule for every external service.

Google and other external service providers may process information using infrastructure outside your country. Their privacy notices describe processing locations and relevant safeguards. The rights and safeguards that apply depend on the data, service and governing law.

13. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or other protections relating to personal information. To make a request about information handled directly through CyberoPlus, contact contact@cyberoplus.com and provide enough detail to identify the relevant correspondence, without sending passwords or sensitive documents. Where Google independently controls processing, the relevant options are available through its privacy controls and policies. If applicable to your situation, you may also contact the relevant data protection authority.

14. Browser controls and advertising choices

You can inspect or delete site data in your browser, restrict cookies according to your browser settings and use Google Ads Settings for advertising preferences. Blocking every cookie may limit website features. Browser deletion alone does not necessarily remove information already processed by an external provider.

15. Children and sensitive information

CyberoPlus publishes general technology education and is not designed specifically as a service directed at young children. Please do not submit sensitive personal data, passwords, financial records or API keys through site forms or email. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data that requires attention, contact us using the address above.

Articles may link to other websites and may contain embedded resources. Those sites are not controlled by CyberoPlus and can apply different privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them.
